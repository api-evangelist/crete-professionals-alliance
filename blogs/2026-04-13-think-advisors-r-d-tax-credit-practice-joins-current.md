Washington, D.C. — Think Advisors, LLC (“Think”), a national tax consulting firm, today announced that its R&D Tax Credit Services practice has joined Current (formerly Crete Professionals Alliance). The R&D Tax Credit Services practice of Think’s wholly owned subsidiary, Black Line Group, LLC, has also joined the alliance.
