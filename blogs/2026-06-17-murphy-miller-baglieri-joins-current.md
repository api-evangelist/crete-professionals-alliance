Glen Rock, NJ — Murphy, Miller & Baglieri (“MMB”), a leading accounting and advisory firm serving the regional market, today announced it has joined Current, formerly Crete Professionals Alliance, named one of Accounting Today’s fastest growing firms. The partnership strengthens MMB’s ability to serve clients with deeper resources, advanced technology, and access to a nationwide network—while maintaining its well-known local brand and leadership team.
